Safety Tips for Night Walks

🔦

Stay in Well-Lit Areas

Choose routes with good street lighting. Avoid shortcuts through dark alleys or isolated areas. Well-lit paths make you more visible and safer.

📱

Share Your Location

Always let someone know where you're going and when you expect to arrive. Use location sharing features to keep trusted contacts informed.

👁️

Walk with Confidence

Stay alert and aware of your surroundings. Walk with purpose and confidence. Avoid distractions like phone use while walking.

💡

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, trust your gut. Change your route, enter a busy place, or call someone. Your instincts are your best defense.

🚨

Emergency Contacts Ready

Keep emergency numbers saved and easily accessible. Set up quick-dial contacts for trusted friends and family members.

👥

Stay Connected

Walking with a companion is always safer. If alone, use safety apps or community watch features to stay connected.
